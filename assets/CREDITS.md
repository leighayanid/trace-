Attribution is recorded here anyway, because knowing where an asset came from matters when
someone later asks whether it can ship.

**Why this one:** near-black rock, white snow and a desaturated navy sky — the TRACE palette
almost exactly, with no off-brand colour. Three other candidates were rejected on sight: a pale
cyan ridge (too light for white type, wrong hue), a silhouette carrying an orange sunset band,
and Mt Fuji with warm town lights across the middle — and, being a recognisable landmark, it
read as a postcard rather than something quiet.

The image is used behind a legibility scrim in `lib/features/splash/splash_screen.dart`. If you
swap the photograph, re-check that scrim: it is tuned to darken the top and bottom bands where
the type sits while leaving the middle clear.
